Amazon A2I — Augmented AI

Amazon Augmented AI (Amazon A2I) is an AWS service that allows you to add human review to ML predictions.

Simple definition

Amazon A2I = Human-in-the-loop for AI/ML predictions.

The key idea is that an ML model makes a prediction automatically, but humans review selected predictions when human judgment is needed.

Why do we need A2I?

ML models aren't always 100% confident.

Suppose an OCR system reads an invoice:

Invoice
   ↓
AI/ML model
   ↓
"Total = ₹82,000"
   ↓
Confidence = 99%

No human review may be necessary.

But:

Invoice
   ↓
AI/ML model
   ↓
"Total = ₹82,000"
   ↓
Confidence = 52%
   ↓
🚨 Human review

A human checks the result and confirms or corrects it.

Human-in-the-loop ⭐⭐⭐

This is the most important concept.

                 ┌── High confidence ──→ Accept prediction
                 │
ML Model ────────┤
                 │
                 └── Low confidence ───→ 👤 Human review

A2I allows you to define conditions that determine when human review should occur.

For example:

If confidence is below 80%, send the result to a human reviewer.

Example with Amazon Textract

Suppose a bank processes loan documents.

📄 Loan document
       ↓
Amazon Textract
       ↓
Extract information
       ↓
Confidence check
       ↓
 ┌─────┴──────┐
 ↓            ↓
High         Low
confidence   confidence
 ↓            ↓
Accept       👤 Human
             review

A2I can create the human-review workflow for the low-confidence results.

This is a very good AIF-C01 scenario.

A2I with Other AWS AI Services

A2I can be used with supported AI/ML workflows where human review is useful.

For example:

Textract
   ↓
Document extraction
   ↓
A2I
   ↓
Human review when required

Or:

Rekognition
   ↓
Image analysis
   ↓
A2I
   ↓
Human review

The exact integration capabilities depend on the service/workflow.

A2I vs Mechanical Turk ⭐⭐⭐

These two are easy to confuse.

Amazon Mechanical Turk

Provides human workers who perform tasks.

Company
   ↓
Mechanical Turk
   ↓
Human workers
   ↓
Complete task
Amazon A2I

Provides the human-review workflow/orchestration for ML predictions.

ML prediction
      ↓
A2I
      ↓
Does this need human review?
      ↓
     Yes
      ↓
👤 Human reviewer
Memory trick

Mechanical Turk = Where humans can perform tasks
A2I = When/how humans review AI results

A2I vs SageMaker Ground Truth

Another distinction:

SageMaker Ground Truth

Mainly about:

Creating labeled datasets for ML training.

Raw data
 ↓
Ground Truth
 ↓
Human labeling
 ↓
Training dataset
A2I

Mainly about:

Reviewing model predictions during an AI/ML workflow.

Trained model
 ↓
Prediction
 ↓
A2I
 ↓
Human review if necessary
Very important:

Ground Truth → Label data for training

A2I → Review AI predictions

🎯 AIF-C01 Exam Scenarios
Scenario 1

An organization wants humans to review ML predictions when the model's confidence falls below a specified threshold.

✅ Amazon A2I

Scenario 2

A bank wants humans to verify information extracted from documents when automated extraction has low confidence.

✅ Amazon A2I

Scenario 3

A company wants humans to label thousands of images to create a training dataset.

✅ Amazon Mechanical Turk / SageMaker Ground Truth, depending on the wording.

Scenario 4

A company wants to automatically extract text and tables from scanned documents.

✅ Amazon Textract

🧠 Final comparison
Service	Main purpose

Amazon A2I	Human review of AI/ML predictions

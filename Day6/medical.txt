AWS provides specialized versions of Transcribe and Comprehend for healthcare.

The easiest way to understand them is:

Transcribe Medical = Medical speech → text 🩺🎤
Comprehend Medical = Medical text → medical insights 🩺📝

1. Amazon Transcribe Medical

Amazon Transcribe Medical is designed to automatically convert medical speech into text.

Example

A doctor says:

"Patient has a history of hypertension and takes 10 milligrams of medication daily."

🎤 Doctor's speech
       ↓
Amazon Transcribe Medical
       ↓
📝 Medical transcript

It is designed to recognize medical terminology better than a general speech-to-text system.

Common use cases
Doctor-patient conversations
Clinical documentation
Medical dictation
Healthcare call-center conversations
Generating medical transcripts
Key exam idea

If the input is audio/speech and the requirement is transcription:

🎤 Transcribe Medical

2. Amazon Comprehend Medical

Amazon Comprehend Medical uses NLP/ML to extract medical information and relationships from unstructured medical text.

Suppose you already have:

"The patient was diagnosed with diabetes and prescribed 500 mg of Metformin."

Comprehend Medical can identify medical entities such as:

Diabetes       → Medical condition
Metformin      → Medication
500 mg         → Dosage

So:

📝 Medical text
       ↓
Amazon Comprehend Medical
       ↓
Medical entities + relationships + attributes
What can Comprehend Medical identify?

It can extract information such as:

Medical conditions
Medications
Dosages
Symptoms
Anatomy
Procedures
Protected health information (PHI) in appropriate contexts
Relationships and attributes associated with medical entities
Example
"The patient has severe chest pain and was prescribed
Aspirin 100 mg."

        ↓

Comprehend Medical

        ↓

Condition → chest pain
Medication → Aspirin
Dosage → 100 mg
⭐ The BIG Difference

This is the most important thing to remember for AIF-C01.

	Transcribe Medical	Comprehend Medical
Input	🎤 Speech/audio	📝 Medical text
Main job	Speech → text	Analyze medical text
Output	Transcript	Medical entities/insights
Example	Doctor dictation → transcript	Transcript → diseases/medications
Technology	Speech recognition / ASR	NLP
Think	"What did the doctor say?"	"What medical information is in what the doctor said?"

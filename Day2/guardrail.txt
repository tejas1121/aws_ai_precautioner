Why do we need embeddings?

Computers and AI models cannot directly compare the meaning of text the way humans do.

Embeddings convert text into numerical vectors that represent its semantic meaning.

Example

Suppose we have:

"How can I reset my password?"

and:

"I forgot my password. How do I change it?"

The words are different, but the meaning is very similar.

An embedding model converts them into vectors:

"How can I reset my password?"
          ↓
[0.21, -0.45, 0.78, 0.12, ...]

"I forgot my password. How do I change it?"
          ↓
[0.19, -0.42, 0.75, 0.15, ...]

Because their meanings are similar, their vectors will generally be close to each other in vector space.

Why is this important for RAG?

This is where embeddings become extremely useful.

Imagine your Knowledge Base contains:

"Customers who forget their credentials can reset their password from the account settings page."

The user asks:

"I can't remember my login password. What should I do?"

There isn't an exact keyword match.

But embeddings allow the system to recognize that:

"can't remember login password"
≈
"forget credentials / reset password"

So the system can retrieve the relevant document.

Very important distinction

Tokenization and embeddings solve different problems:

Concept	Purpose
Tokenization	Breaks text into tokens that the model can process
Embedding	Converts text into numerical vectors representing meaning
Vector DB	Stores and searches those vectors
